Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9/7/2026

Welcome to Tool Studio. We take your privacy seriously. This policy details what data we collect and how we handle it.

Tool Studio is an AI-assisted content creation toolkit for writing, marketing, SEO, prompt and image tools. This page explains what information is collected, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have over it. Tool Studio is provided by its developer (Issac Ashraf). Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@issactools.com. ## 1. Overview - Using the tools requires a free account (email address). Signing in only identifies you; the content you create stays on your device and is never uploaded to your account. - We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not train AI models on your content. ## 2. What we collect and where it lives - **Account data (cloud).** When you create an account you provide an email address and a password. The password is stored only as a salted, hashed credential by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never see or store your plain-text password. A minimal profile row (user ID and a display name derived from your email prefix) is created so the app can identify you when you sign in. - **Generated content (your device).** Tool Studio does not upload your generations or preferences to your account. History, Gallery, favorites and preferences are stored only in your device's local storage and are removed when you clear it or delete the app. - **Prompts & outputs (transient).** When you run a tool, the text or image prompt you enter is sent to a third-party AI provider solely to produce your result. The result is returned to you; Tool Studio does not retain the prompt or output beyond fulfilling the request, and they are not used to improve any AI model. - **Usage logs (server).** Request logs record which tool was used, the model, latency, token counts and a rate-limited IP address so we can operate the service and enforce fair-use limits. Logs never include your prompt text, generated content, email address or password. - **What we do not collect:** no precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, purchase history or payment information. No advertising identifiers are used. ## 3. Third-party processors - **Supabase (supabase.com)** hosts account authentication and the database that stores account profiles. Row Level Security is enabled so each user can only access their own data. - **OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) and the AI model providers it routes to** (which may include OpenAI, Google, Meta, Nvidia and others) receive the text you submit to a text-generation tool in order to generate the response, under each provider's terms. - **Pollinations.ai** receives image prompts to render generated images. Image prompts can be visible inside the public image URL returned to you, so avoid including private information in image prompts. - **Google Fonts** are loaded from Google's CDN; a standard font request (including IP address) is made by your browser. No account data is involved. - If you are in the EEA/UK, note that some providers may operate outside the EEA; by using the tools you consent to this processing. ## 4. How we use your information - To provide the service: authenticating you and generating the content you request. - To operate and secure the service: rate limiting, abuse prevention and diagnosing failures from aggregate usage logs. - To contact you about your account: confirmation, password-reset and similar transactional emails through Supabase when you request them. We never send marketing email and never sell or rent your address. ## 5. Retention - Sign-in credentials and your profile are kept while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your credential and profile. Content on your device is not affected by server deletion; use "Clear device data" (Account & Data) or uninstall the app to remove it. - Usage logs are kept in a rotating file (5 MB × 3 backups) and are removed automatically as the log rotates; they contain no content. - Prompts and outputs sent to AI providers are governed by those providers' retention policies. ## 6. Your choices, account deletion & your rights - **Device data:** open Account & Data and choose "Clear device data" to remove history, gallery and preferences stored on the current device. - **Account deletion:** open Account & Data → "Delete account & data". Deletion is immediate and permanent — your email credential and profile are removed from the authentication provider and you are signed out. - If in-app deletion is ever unavailable, email privacy@issactools.com from the address registered to the account with the subject "Account deletion request"; we process it within 30 days. - You may also request access to, a copy of, or correction of your account data, or withdraw consent for processing, by emailing privacy@issactools.com; we respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA/UK you may also complain to your local data-protection authority. ## 7. Security - Passwords are hashed and stored by Supabase Auth; we never handle plain-text passwords. - Account data is protected by database Row Level Security and authenticated API endpoints. - The backend never returns raw error traces, and API keys live only in server-side environment files — never in the app. - No security measure is perfect. Please use a strong, unique password and contact us about any suspected issue. ## 8. AI output disclaimer & responsible use - Generated content may contain errors, inaccuracies, hallucinations or unintended bias. Always review and fact-check AI output before publishing, sending or acting on it. - AI outputs are provided "as is", without warranties of accuracy, originality or fitness for a particular purpose. - You are responsible for the content you produce, including compliance with copyright, marketing and anti-spam laws (for example CAN-SPAM and GDPR) where you operate. - Do not paste confidential, personal or sensitive information into tools if you are not comfortable sending it to third-party AI providers, and do not generate illegal, defamatory, hateful or otherwise harmful content. - If you use generated images commercially, verify the image provider's licensing terms first. ## 9. Children Tool Studio is intended for adults and professionals, not children. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital-consent age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact privacy@issactools.com and we will delete it. ## 10. Terms of use & fair use - The service is provided free of charge. Fair-use limits apply to AI generations (for example a per-device hourly limit); exceeding them returns a friendly message asking you to try again later. No paid tier currently exists. - You may not resell access to the app or its API, use it to build a competing service, automate it to bypass fair-use limits, or use it to send spam. - We may change, limit or discontinue features at any time. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. - If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and, where practical, notify you in the app.
